Case study 01

Asian Immunization Program Analysis

Portfolio / Technical BI project

Goal

Turn a raw, large-scale public health dataset (34 million rows across 20 countries) into an interactive BI solution that non-technical stakeholders can use to evaluate vaccination distribution trends and spot critical supply chain deficits.

My contribution

Sole end-to-end BI developer. I designed the relational schema, wrote optimised SQL to structure and aggregate the records, engineered the Power BI semantic model and DAX measures, and built the multi-page dashboard.

Outcome

A multi-page interactive dashboard with self-service filtering. It surfaced under-performance in BCG vaccine distribution alongside an Influenza peak of 5.68 million doses.

Case study 02

Patient Appointment No-Show Analytics: True North Health Network

Case study · June–July 2026

Goal

Find the behavioural and operational drivers behind missed appointments across branches and specialties, and recommend scheduling changes that reduce lost clinic capacity.

My contribution

Sole data analyst. I ingested and cleaned 1,758 multi-branch records, built diagnostic models, engineered behavioural risk flags, designed the interactive executive dashboard and wrote the operational recommendations.

Outcome

Booking gaps over 30 days lifted default risk to 28%. Patients with three or more prior no-shows defaulted 46% of the time. The UK branch (21%) and Dermatology (34%) were flagged as priorities for automated SMS reminders and shorter booking intervals.
