Case study 02

Patient Appointment No-Show Analytics: True North Health Network

Capstone case study · BA Health Tech Pod Orion · Jun–Jul 2026

Goal

Investigate the behavioural and operational drivers behind missed appointments across branches and specialties, and give data-backed scheduling recommendations to reduce lost clinic capacity.

My contribution

Data Analyst in a team capstone (Pod Orion). I ingested and cleaned 1,758 multi-branch records, built diagnostic models, engineered behavioural risk flags, designed the interactive executive dashboard, and contributed to the stakeholder presentation and management action plan.

Outcome

422 of 1,758 appointments were missed (24%) across 8 countries and 7 specialties. Booking gaps of 31+ days raised default risk to 29%, and patients with 3+ prior no-shows defaulted 37% of the time. Dermatology, UK & France and Eastgate Specialist Center were identified as priority targets.

24%overall no-show rate (422 missed)
29%default risk, gaps of 31+ days
37%default rate, 3+ prior no-shows

Estimate from the analysis: 100+ appointments a month could be recovered if branches matched Germany’s 18% benchmark. Branch-level modelling gave 28% for the 31+ day gap.

Case study 03

Drug Delivery Process Improvement: MedBridge Pharmacy

Volunteer / unpaid · Amdari · Sep–Oct 2026

Goal

Address routine prescription delivery delays (up to 7 days against a 3-day target), low real-time order status visibility (capped at 40%) and a 12% order capture error rate.

My contribution

Sole Business Analyst on this voluntary, unpaid case study. I ran an end-to-end root-cause analysis with the 6M Ishikawa (Fishbone) framework, mapped current-state (AS-IS) and future-state (TO-BE) workflows in draw.io / BPMN, and authored the Business Requirements Document (BRD).

Outcome

Causal drivers structured across People, Process, Technology, Data, Inputs and Policy, and a BRD translating stakeholder needs into functional and non-functional requirements. It specifies a validated online order intake form, synchronised inventory checking and automated courier dispatch alerts, designed to raise order visibility from 40% to 100% and eliminate intake capture errors.
